01 / PROFILE

The Mind Behind Innovation

TECHNOLOGY ENTREPRENEUR

Elon Musk is a technology entrepreneur and business leader known for founding, leading and investing in companies focused on transportation, space technology, artificial intelligence and digital communication. Born in Pretoria, South Africa, he developed an interest in computing and technology at an early age. He later moved to North America and studied at the University of Pennsylvania.

Musk became an early internet entrepreneur through companies including Zip2 and X.com, which contributed to the development of PayPal. He later became associated with SpaceX, Tesla, Neuralink and other technology ventures. SpaceX focuses on reusable launch systems and space transportation, while Tesla has helped accelerate the adoption of electric vehicles.

His work has also included satellite internet, brain-computer interfaces and artificial intelligence. His projects are characterized by ambitious engineering goals and an emphasis on developing technologies intended to have large-scale impact.
